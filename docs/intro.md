# CV

## Purpose of the Document

Append-only living document. A set of facts reflecting personal growth in software. Not a resume.
Not a cover letter. The purpose of the document is to record history without positioning, metrics,
or marketing, and to make it possible to generate a consistent projection in the form of a Resume.

- `CV (Curriculum Vitae) - course of life (Latin)` - a chronicle of biographical facts.
- `Resume - a summary (French)` - a condensed projection of a biography tailored to the
  requirements of a specific vacancy.

The document may have an artistic style.
