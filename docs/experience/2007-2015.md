# 2007 - 2015

## 2007 - First Introduction to the Web

During my student years I had a part-time job. My employer ordered an online store from a web studio.
That sparked my interest in how websites work. That's how I found w3schools and started going through
the tutorials.

I studied for about 3-4 months. As a pet project I finally built my first website - a typical resume
site with a gallery. Then came difficulties with JS and confusion about how and where to apply that
knowledge. It felt like there was a chasm between a tutorial and real work. I abandoned learning in
this field for years and went back to working and studying economics.

## 2007 - 2015 - Occasional Web Experiments

From time to time I returned to the tutorials. I experimented with markup, animation, and scripts.
I built simple landing pages and spent a lot of time on front-end layout. In parallel, I earned a
degree in economics and worked outside of IT.

Studies took up a lot of time, and I had to work. Money and a job were needed here and now, so I
couldn't get into searching for web jobs or further learning.

I kept thinking that only professional programmers could work in this field.
