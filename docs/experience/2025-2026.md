# 2025 - PRESENT

## Leaving the Studio

I felt I had outgrown the team I was in. The company could offer neither a higher position nor pay
on par with big tech. I wanted to absorb new experience. The company was actively introducing AI
into its processes - to the point of having developers retrain into prompt engineering.

I see AI as just another tool in the system - and far from the most important one. It's another
possible piece of the product delivery process, not a replacement for thinking.

## New Company

After leaving the studio, I joined a product company in digital healthcare. The company builds online
doctor appointment booking: scheduling services for patients and tools for clinics.

## Promotions Admin Panel

A major rework of the existing admin panel for running and building promotions. I introduced
architectural boundaries and loosened coupling between modules in the project. I replaced custom
solutions with battle-tested libraries for forms and tables.

## Main Company Website

Developed features on the main website: promotions, working with appointment booking slots.

## Design System

Made changes to the internal component design system.

## Partners Admin Panel

Set up the build and modules from scratch for the partners admin panel project.

## Online Booking Widget

Set up the build and structure from scratch and fully implemented the online doctor appointment
widget. Completely rewrote the old solution, respecting strict architectural boundary rules. This
created huge potential for easy future maintenance of the program.

## Starter Templates

Developed starter templates for new projects on Next.js. The template included everything: root
config files, architecture decision records, convention files, playground modules with examples.

## Initiatives and Talks

I drive initiatives: frontend architecture, higher review quality, and code quality, plus
systematizing initiative documents.
