## Brainful

Released the open-source project Brainful - https://brainfuljs.dev/. It's a playground for sharpening
engineering skills in building applications. The goal is to free yourself from the intrusive
constructs of "magical" frameworks, keep observable state in your hands, work with a DI container,
and build proper states for efficient UI updates.
